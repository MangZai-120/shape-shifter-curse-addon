### v8.0.0

Released November 16, 2026 for Minecraft 1.20.1 Fabric and SSC 1.10.0.

- Added Fluorescent Axolotl, Aling, Wind Spirit, Nova, Frostspine, Nightmare, Moon Weaver, and Salticidae. [Upgrade Familiar and Upgrade Axolotl](../sp_forms/overview.md) have their own XP and talent trees.
- Added [26 spells across seven schools](../spells/catalog.md), Moon Dust Spellbooks, the Spell Research Table, and the Spell Preparation Table. Scrolls can be analyzed, crafted, upgraded, and imprinted using world-specific rune rules.
- Added [disintegration](../spells/collapse.md), player casting progression, and [elemental anomalies and reactions](../spells/anomalies.md).
- Added the Instinct Anchor and release item, energy machinery, potion test tubes, and Moon Dust Potions. Multiplayer balance data can be retried on an active connection so restricted abilities can resume after synchronization.
