# Trinkets

This mod adds form-exclusive trinkets, divided into equippable and non-equippable trinkets.

***

## Equippable Trinkets

### Blue Fire Amulet

**Blue Fire Amulet**

Exclusive trinket of the SP Familiar Fox form.

* **Form**: SP Familiar Fox
* **Effect**: Empowers the Blue Fire Ring

***

### Blizzard Tank

**Blizzard Tank**

Exclusive trinket of the SP Snow Fox form, also serves as the ammo pack for the Snowball Launcher.

* **Form**: SP Snow Fox
* **Capacity**: 64 rounds
* **Auto Reload**: When equipped, refills the Snowball Launcher with 2 rounds per second
* **Self Charging**: Automatically restores internal frost reserve (1 per 2 seconds), no fuel needed

***

### Frost Amulet

**Frost Amulet**

Exclusive trinket of the SP Snow Fox form.

* **Form**: SP Snow Fox
* **Effect**: After the Ice Orb hits, it splits into 2 self-tracking ice shards that attack nearby creatures.

***

### Lifesaving Cat Tail

**Lifesaving Cat Tail**

Exclusive trinket of the Wild Cat (SP) form.

* **Form**: Wild Cat (SP)
* **Effect**: Prevent one fatal hit and grant True Invisibility

***

### Dramatic Bell

**Dramatic Bell**

Exclusive trinket of the SP Familiar Fox form.

* **Form**: SP Familiar Fox
* **Effect**: When HP gets too low, teleport to a relatively safe nearby location

***

### Active Coral Necklace

**Active Coral Necklace**

Exclusive trinket of the SP Axolotl form.

* **Form**: SP Axolotl
* **Effect**: Converts part of the Playing Dead recovery into a max-HP increase

***

### Blood Garnet

**Blood Garnet**

Exclusive trinket of the Desmodus (Vampire Bat) form.

* **Form**: Desmodus (Vampire Bat)
* **Effect**: Blood Thirst gains **+50% faster**
* **Cost**: Out-of-combat decay **+50%**, and passively loses 1 Blood Thirst per second

***

### Bloodlust Ring

**Bloodlust Ring**

Exclusive trinket of the Desmodus (Vampire Bat) form.

* **Form**: Desmodus (Vampire Bat)
* **Effect**: **+15% extra lifesteal** at high Blood Thirst stages
* **Cost**: When at full health, lifesteal backfires for 1 true damage per second

***

### Humus Ring

**Humus Ring**

Exclusive trinket of the Parasitic Fruit Bat form, strengthening the debuff route.

* **Form**: Parasitic Fruit Bat
* **Effect**: Hostile debuff fruit duration **+50%**
* **Cost**: Allied buff fruit duration **-30%**

***

### Twin Pod

**Twin Pod**

Exclusive trinket of the Parasitic Fruit Bat form, strengthening coverage.

* **Form**: Parasitic Fruit Bat
* **Effect**: Each cast also parasitizes the nearest second target
* **Cost**: Doubles Seed Charge cost and adds **+1s** cooldown

***

### Axolotl Humidifier

**Axolotl Humidifier**

The [Axolotl Humidifier](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/920744.html) is a belt accessory for Abyssal Overlord, Upgrade Axolotl, Fluorescent, and Aling. It works automatically while equipped. Hold and right-click to check its charge.

| Level | Maximum moisture restored per second | Charge capacity |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| I | 2% | 90 minutes |
| II | 3% | 135 minutes |
| III | 4% | 180 minutes |

Moisture restoration rounds up and spends one second of charge when moisture is below maximum. In rain, the humidifier pauses its restoration and replenishes one second of charge every two seconds. Equipping level III grants 15% extra damage by default, even when its charge is depleted.

The level-I recipe uses seven Iron Ingots, a Wet Sponge in the center, and Redstone Dust at the bottom. It starts with 600 seconds of charge. Craft it with an Axolotl Bucket for a full charge. For upgrades, put the old item in the center. Use Wet Sponges in the corners and Gold Ingots along the edges for I→II, or Hearts of the Sea in the corners and Diamonds along the edges for II→III. Upgrades preserve remaining charge.

***

## Non-Equippable Trinkets

### Snowball Launcher

**Snowball Launcher**

A ranged weapon for the SP Snow Fox form.

* **Form**: SP Snow Fox
* **Fire rate**: 1 round per 0.35 seconds (heavy-machine-gun tier)
* **Magazine**: 20 rounds
* **Ammo**: Consumes snowballs from inventory
* **Trait**: High knockback pressure
* **Synergy**: Pair with the [Blizzard Tank](#blizzard-tank) for automatic reload
