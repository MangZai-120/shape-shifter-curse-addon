# Salticidae (Jumping Spider)

Salticidae is the Evolution Stone SP branch of the spider stage III, centered on **charged pounce kills + safety-silk recall** as its mobility-output loop, backed by venom AoE. Walls and the air are its true hunting grounds.

!!! warning "Irreversible"
    Unlike vanilla SSC SP forms, all SP forms in this addon **cannot** be reverted with inhibitors.

## Active Skills

### Pounce Kill (Primary Skill Key)

* Hold to charge — longer charge = longer target lock range: base **3 blocks**, up to **16 blocks** at full 60-tick (3s) charge.
* Movement halved while charging; pounce candidates glow red, nearest to crosshair glows green (self only).
* Release to pounce in a parabola toward the locked target (or crosshair point if none); higher jumps for longer distances; tracks moving targets' intercept points; escaping beyond tolerance (proportional to jump distance) whiffs.
* Hit deals **8 physical damage + Poison II 160 ticks (8s) + root 7 ticks (0.35s)**.
* CD: hit **240 ticks (12s)**, whiff **160 ticks (8s)**.

### Safety Silk Recall

Within **160 ticks (8s)** after a pounce, press the primary key again to zip back to the launch anchor along the safety silk (**ignores cooldown**); silk up to **48 blocks**, breaks if blocked.

### Venom (Secondary Skill Key)

* Base: 4 magic damage + Poison I 300 ticks (15s) to all creatures in a 2×2×2 area ahead.
* Venom Dash (if safety silk is attached within 8s after a pounce): dash 6 blocks along the crosshair; hitting an enemy deals 2 magic damage and stops, then 6 magic damage + Poison II 300 ticks (15s) to all within 3 blocks.
* CD **200 ticks (10s)**.

## Passive Talents

### Bounce Instinct

Normal jump height ×1.65, double jump in air, no fall damage from any height.

### Venom Fangs

Melee hits inject Poison I 100 ticks (5s); with the Venom Gland trinket it becomes Poison II 60 ticks (3s). Whitelisted individuals are immune.

### Predator's Might

+2 damage on melee attacks.

### Inherited Passives

Arthropod group (spiders friendly, iron golems hostile), auxiliary forelimbs (tools, shields, extra totem slot), night vision & poison immunity, climbing (free movement in webs).
