# Nightmare — Dream Hunter

Nightmare is the Moon Essence Cross SP branch of the special wild cat form (the stage before Moonlight Phantom), stacking a full **dream-hunting system** on the feline stalker base: put targets to sleep, terrorize them with compressed vision and heartbeat dread, punish them for turning around, and convert executions into sustain — a classic high-risk high-reward assassin.

!!! warning "Irreversible"
    Unlike vanilla SSC SP forms, all SP forms in this addon **cannot** be reverted with inhibitors.

***

## Dreaming

Deal ten cumulative damage to the same target.

The target enters a fixed ten-second dream. You see a pink outline; a player target sees a pink screen veil.

Further hits do not refresh the timer and instead trigger Dream Devour. Each nightmare owns its own dream ledger. Dreaming does not immobilize every action.

## Dream Debuff Guard

Dream an enemy, then let that enemy apply a harmful effect to you.

An enemy dreaming under your ownership cannot apply source-attributed harmful statuses or supported highlights to you.

The protected direction is dreamer to nightmare. Your debuffs on the prey still work; awake enemies are not blocked. The same real player applies Slowness in both comparisons.

## Fear

With targets dreaming under your ownership, press the primary key.

Apply fifteen seconds of Fear, twenty-percent Slowness, a sixteen-block pink fog and heartbeat. Dream duration stays locked back to twenty seconds.

The nightmare is hidden beyond sixteen blocks; inside, it alternates two seconds hidden and 0.6 seconds visible. Attacking reveals it for 1.5 seconds. Fear ends with forced waking and twenty seconds of dream immunity without damage accumulation. Normal cooldown is twenty seconds; no targets means no cooldown.

## Fear First Hit and Aggro

Apply Fear to a dreaming target, then attack repeatedly.

The first hit from a nightmare or its whitelisted ally deals double damage during this Fear. Later hits resolve normally.

The doubling is consumed once per Fear. The recording also shows targeting suppression: feared mobs cannot maintain aggro on the casting nightmare. Compare equal iron-sword hits on the same unarmoured target.

## Spook

With targets dreaming under your ownership, press the secondary key.

A player sees and hears a private ghost creeper behind them. Ignoring it for three seconds causes no damage. Hitting it summons a ghost cat that deals twelve magic damage.

A mob takes twelve magic damage directly and its dream resets to twenty seconds. Cooldown is eight seconds; no targets means no cooldown. Separate recordings show ignoring, attacking and the mob result. No Nightmare Ring is equipped.

## Dream Devour

While injured, hit an already dreaming target, then kill it.

Damage to an already dreaming target heals fifteen percent of the damage passed to the settlement hook. Killing that target heals four additional health.

The hit that first creates the dream does not heal in advance. The recording separates a normal hit from a later kill, with natural regeneration disabled.

## Dream Spellcasting

Cast equal-level spells on awake targets and targets dreaming under your ownership.

If the target is still dreaming under your ownership on impact, spell damage is multiplied by 1.35. Harmful duration is multiplied by 1.35 and rounded up to ticks; amplifier is unchanged.

Fire Bolt and Curse Mark compare damage and harmful duration separately. Ordinary melee, Spook, potions and beneficial effects do not gain this bonus. Another nightmare's dream does not qualify.

## Void Affinity

Cast equal-level Void Devour as a human and as a nightmare against matching targets.

Nightmare has a separate 1.15 multiplier to Void damage. A qualifying book-cast hit refunds fifty percent of that cast cost, reducing paid collapse in the current system.

Refunds share a one-second player gate and a per-cast budget. Awake targets isolate Void affinity here; the preceding lesson compares the additional thirty-five percent dream bonus.

## Cursed Moon Resonance

Accumulate damage and cast Fear during ordinary time and an actual Cursed Moon night.

Dream threshold falls from ten to six, and Fear cooldown falls from twenty to fourteen seconds.

A daytime iron-sword hit of six is below the ordinary threshold. The Cursed Moon night also has the night damage bonus, allowing one hit to cross six. The recording shows actual server thresholds and cooldowns; ordinary night is not treated as a Cursed Moon.

## Phantom Affinity

Compare phantom targeting against a human and a nightmare, including after being hit.

A phantom refuses to target the nightmare, including retaliation target selection after the nightmare attacks.

This is specific phantom targeting protection. The observer footage shows both forms and the real aggro target.

## Night Hunter

Walk and attack matching targets by day and at night.

At night, movement speed is multiplied by 1.2 and damage gains one point. The old daytime slowness penalty no longer applies.

Night covers game time 13000 through 23000, with roughly one-second update granularity. Keep hunger conditions equal and distinguish inherited movement bonuses from the night multiplier. This uses attributes rather than repeatedly refreshed Speed effects.

## Feral Diet

Eat one raw beef from matching hunger and saturation starting values.

Raw-meat food and saturation modifiers are each increased by one hundred percent.

Both modifiers enter the calculation: the human gains 3 hunger and 1.8 saturation, while Nightmare gains 6 hunger and 7.2 saturation. This does not double every food. Inherited movement and jumps retain their hunger requirements.

## Enhanced Night Vision

Enter a matching enclosed dark room as the upstream Feral Cat SP and Nightmare.

Night vision rises from upstream Cat Vision strength 0.4 to automatically enabled full strength 1.0.

Use identical graphics settings and no Night Vision potion. Climbing, fall immunity and inherited baseline movement and jumps are not demonstrated again.

## Maximum Health Change

Observe actual maximum health as a human, upstream Feral Cat SP and Nightmare.

This form subtracts four maximum health. With the ordinary twenty-health base, Nightmare has sixteen and upstream Feral Cat SP eighteen.

Show the additional cost introduced by the addon. Inherited leg and foot armour restrictions remain text-only; Lifesaving Tail accessory effects are skipped.
