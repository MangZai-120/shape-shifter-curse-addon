# Nightmare — Dream Hunter

Nightmare is the Moon Essence Cross SP branch of the special wild cat form (the stage before Moonlight Phantom), stacking a full **dream-hunting system** on the feline stalker base: put targets to sleep, terrorize them with compressed vision and heartbeat dread, punish them for turning around, and convert executions into sustain — a classic high-risk high-reward assassin.

## Core Mechanic: Dream Initiation

* Accumulating **10 damage** (6 on Cursed Moon nights) on one target puts it into the **Dreaming** sleep state.
* Hit-triggered dreaming lasts a fixed **200 ticks (10s)**; dreaming targets glow pink for the Nightmare only.
* Dreaming enemies cannot perceive your malice — all negative effects your side applies (roots, highlights, etc.) are nullified while they dream.
* Further hits don't extend dreaming; they trigger Dream Feed lifesteal instead.
* After Terror ends, targets forcibly wake with a **400-tick (20s)** dream immunity (damage doesn't accumulate during it).

## Active Skills

### Terror (Primary Skill Key)

* All dreaming enemies: vision compressed to a **16-block** pink fog shell, 20% slowed, with a guardian heartbeat that grows louder as you approach.
* Lasts **300 ticks (15s**; **20.25s** with the Nightmare Ring); dreaming duration is locked (effectively extended control).
* The first hit from you or your whitelisted allies on a terrified target is **doubled**.
* You are fully invisible to terrified targets beyond 16 blocks; within 16 blocks you pulse (2s hidden / 0.6s visible); your attacks reveal you for 1.5s.
* Terrified creatures cannot see you or hold aggro; after being hit they get only a 2s retaliation window before aggro vanishes.
* CD **400 ticks (20s**; 280 ticks / 14s on Cursed Moon nights).

### Fright (Secondary Skill Key)

* Behind a dreaming enemy, summon a phantom creeper **only it can see** (a real entity, fully invisible to others); the fuse closes in 3 seconds then detonates harmlessly.
* If the target turns and attacks the creeper, a phantom wild cat bursts from the smoke and strikes its face (**12 magic damage**), dissolving into fireworks only the target sees.
* Against dreaming-but-not-terrified creatures, the invisible cat bites directly (**12 magic damage**) and resets dreaming to 400 ticks (20s).
* CD **160 ticks (8s)**.

## Passive Talents

### Dream Feed

15% of damage dealt to dreaming targets converts to your health; kills in dreams devour the last dream for 4 extra HP.

### Phantom Affinity

Phantoms never hunt you — even when attacked, they merely retreat.

### Cursed Moon Resonance

On Cursed Moon nights: dream threshold drops from 10 to 6 damage; Terror CD drops from 20s to 14s.

### Inherited Wild Cat Passives

Silent steps, creeper deterrence, no fall damage, phantom intimidation, night speed (Speed I) & night vision, daytime sluggishness (Slowness I), raw-meat diet, free climbing, feline-friendly, Lifesaver Cat Tail trinket slot, etc.
