# Fluorescent — Deep Sea Glow

Fluorescent is the Evolution Stone SP branch of the axolotl line — an amphibious form built around **moisture management, tidal control, and charged laser bursts**. Nearly unbeatable in water, it must carefully manage moisture on land.

!!! warning "Irreversible"
    Unlike vanilla SSC SP forms, all SP forms in this addon **cannot** be reverted with inhibitors.

## Active Skills

### Tidal Surge (Secondary Skill Key)

* Charge **25 ticks (1.25s)** with 50% slow; then fire a tidal ball flying 4 blocks/s for 8 seconds.
* Press again mid-flight to trigger **deceleration & tether**: a 6-block water field centered on the ball tethers non-whitelisted creatures for **8.5s**; tethered targets beyond 6 blocks are pulled back linearly.
* CD **160 ticks (8s)**.
* With the Sea Crystal Pendant, the charge becomes a one-shot dash-blast ball — sprint acceleration, AoE damage and slow on explosion.

### Magic Circle Laser (Primary Skill Key)

* Base: charge **7s** fully immobilized, then fire a **32-block** straight laser dealing ~**120** magic damage to non-whitelisted targets along the line.
* With the Sea Crystal Pendant: three circles unfold behind you; within a **5s window**, each press fires a compact laser — 24-block range, 0.75-block radius, 12 physical × 4 hits per laser, +6s CD each; window ends after 3 lasers or 5s idle. -50% movement throughout, 10° auto-aim cone. CD cap **1200 ticks (60s)**.

***

## Passive Talents

### Slime Membrane

**20%** chance to fully dodge physical attacks (melee, projectiles, thorns, cactus, darts); excludes magic, fire, lava, suffocation, hunger, void. Passive defense — ignores whitelist.

### Mucus Slow Fall

Complete immunity to fall damage.

### Moisture Drain

* Out of water and no rain: **3 moisture per second** (1.5%/s).
* At zero moisture: 2 dehydration damage per second (armor-piercing).
* On fire with ≥10 moisture: auto-extinguish for 10 moisture per second.

### Water Affinity

Inherited axolotl instincts — water breathing, underwater vision, swim speed, carnivorous diet, no helmet.

***

## Story Form: Aling

After evolving into Fluorescent, collect the 7 *Azure Port Chronicle* books from loot chests, earn the "Fluorescent" advancement, then sleep on a Cursed Moon night to permanently transform into the story form **Aling**: slower land moisture drain (~25%), innate triple-laser (1.2× range/damage/hitbox of pendant Fluorescent), +20% tidal ball duration with damage-over-time while tethered, +2 max HP, and free Moon Essence Cross reversion to Fluorescent.
