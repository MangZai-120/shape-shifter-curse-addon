# Moon Weaver

Moon Weaver is the Moon Essence Cross SP branch of the spider stage III, layering **web control, silk swing and double-jump mobility** on top of vanilla web hunting. The rhythm: control the field with webs first, then harvest with mobility.

!!! warning "Irreversible"
    Unlike vanilla SSC SP forms, all SP forms in this addon **cannot** be reverted with inhibitors.

## Active Skills

### Silk Spinning & Webbing (Primary Skill Key)

* Hold to charge a web projectile — 3 charge tiers (1s / 2s / 3s cap); higher tier = wider web.
* Attack mode: on hit, lay decelerating surface webs in radius **3 / 5 / 6 blocks** by tier.
* Sneak + double-press to toggle **Path / Attack** modes; Path mode lays silk bridges underfoot to cross gaps.
* Costs **6** silk mana upfront + **0.25**/tick while charging.

### Silk Swing (Secondary Skill Key)

* Fire a parabolic silk bolt; hitting a block starts a **swing** (silk up to 32 blocks, 2 mana per block of rope paid out).
* Hitting a creature instantly costs 8 extra mana to **tether and drag** it toward you (leash up to 16 blocks, non-extendable).
* Tethered targets gain a highlight outline — green for allies, blue for enemies, visible only to the caster.

## Passive Talents

### Web Affinity

Standing on cobwebs or your own membranes: +20% movement, 1 mana/s regen, -20% damage taken.

### Moon Venom Erosion

Enemies within 8 blocks caught in your Web Bound or cocoon states are continuously poisoned (Poison I, refreshed every 3s with 4s duration). Spiders and allay-line forms are immune.

### Cocoon Hunter

Melee attacks against cocooned (fully immobilized) targets deal +15% damage and restore silk mana.

### Silk Double Jump

One extra jump per airtime, with a sprinting lunge; sprint double jumps stir extra mist and purple particles.

### Web Membrane

Thin surface-layer blocks laid by Moon Weaver:

* Non-immune creatures touching them are slowed 30% and inflicted with **Spider Web Bound** (-50% speed, linked Mining Fatigue I & Weakness I, milk cannot cleanse, fades ~5s after leaving) for 5s.
* Membranes dissolve after 60~90s, wash away in water, and are flammable with fast spread.
* Spiders, allay-line forms, and whitelisted allies/pets are immune.

### Inherited Spider Passives

Wall climbing, free movement in webs, night vision, poison immunity, arthropod group (spiders friendly, iron golems hostile), fall protection, auto-feeding, edible string & spider cocoons for mana, extra arms for tools.
