# Upgrade Familiar — Standalone Evolution Line

[Open the gameplay tutorial](../../tutorials/upgrade-familiar/index.html)

Evolution points come from milestones in an independent evolution level; there are no additional kill or gathering quests. Every two newly earned vanilla XP count as one evolution XP, with a level cap of 50. Open the evolution page in the Shape Shifter Book, select a node and confirm its purchase. Each main node costs one point, all listed prerequisites are required, and final branches cost no additional points.

Upgrade Familiar is a **standalone mid-state form** based on the vanilla familiar. Choosing it leaves the vanilla fixed evolution path in favor of the SSCA evolution-tree point system, unlocking abilities level by level and finally transforming into one of two SP forms.

***

| Item | Description |

| Classification | Standalone starting form (SSCA evolution line) |

***

## How to Obtain

Not evolved from the vanilla familiar with an item — instead, when first opening the Shapeshifter's Book before treading the vanilla curse path, enter the "SSCA Evolution Line" in the selection UI and pick Upgrade Familiar.

***

## Core Resource: Mana

* The Mana System node grants a mana bar (**initial cap 33 with natural regeneration; cap 103 with all main talents**).
* Later nodes raise the cap and regen.
* Mana can also be siphoned by sneaking and interacting with creatures, with rewards depending on target type and talents; skills cannot be cast when empty.

***

## Skill Tree Nodes

Gain 1 point each at levels **5/10/15/20/30/40/45** (7 total):

| Node | Effect | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mana System | Mana bar, damage-reduction shield, mana siphon | — |
| Spark | Primary: 11 Mana, close-range damage and five seconds of ignition, two-second cooldown | Mana System |
| Fire Rocket | Fireball with a stick; explosive fireball with talisman paper | Mana System |
| Buff Immunity | Resists specified debuffs; fire protection depends on Mana conditions | Mana System |
| Alchemy | Craft strong splash potions from materials | Spark + Fire Rocket |
| Spirit Sight | Outline glow for creatures in range | Buff Immunity |
| Fire Ring | Active: replaces Spark — larger, stronger, longer ignition | Alchemy + Spirit Sight |

With every main talent unlocked at level **50**, the two final branches (Spirit Realm Lord / Mancianima) unlock automatically.

***

## Final Transformation

With the trunk fully unlocked (up to Fire Ring) and branches unlocked at level 50:

| Branch | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spirit Realm Lord | Moon Essence Cross on a Cursed Moon night | [SP Familiar Fox](sp_familiar_fox.md) |
| Mancianima | Evolution Stone | [Mancianima](sp_familiar_fox_mancianima.md) |

***

## Costs & Limits

* All actives depend on mana — empty mana means no skills.
* Familiar-based body: no leggings/boots, sluggish in water, less saturation from food.
