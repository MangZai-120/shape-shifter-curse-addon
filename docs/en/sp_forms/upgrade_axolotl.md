# Upgrade Axolotl — Standalone Evolution Line

Upgrade Axolotl is a **standalone mid-state form** based on the vanilla axolotl, focused on aquatic combat. Like Upgrade Familiar, it leaves the fixed path for the SSCA evolution-tree point system.

***

## Form Profile

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Classification | Standalone starting form (SSCA evolution line) |
| Combat role | Growth-type aquatic combat / Custom build |

***

## How to Obtain

A standalone starting form: when first opening the Shapeshifter's Book, enter the "SSCA Evolution Line" in the selection UI and pick Upgrade Axolotl.

***

## Core Resource: Moisture

Reuses the vanilla moisture system. Out of water, moisture drains continuously; before unlocking the "Become Water" node, land drainage is **1.5×**. Running dry causes weakness and health loss.

***

## Skill Tree Nodes

Gain 1 point each at levels **5/10/15/20/30/40** (6 total):

| Node | Effect | Prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aquatic Adaptation | Underwater night vision, swim speed, surface breach | — |
| Current Propulsion | Land sprint speed up; sprint jumps gain extra lunge and splash | — |
| Current Dash | Sprint + sneak triggers a forward burst | Aquatic Adaptation + Current Propulsion |
| Water Spear Throw | Primary: leap back-up, charge ~1.35s, throw a straight spear — 12 direct damage + 5 splash damage in 2 blocks, ~6% moisture, 8s CD | Current Dash |
| Vortex Channeling | Secondary: channel 3s (slowed) to heal 6 hearts, gain 2 golden hearts (30s) at the end, no moisture cost, 8s CD | Current Dash |
| Become Water | Removes the extra land moisture drain | Water Spear Throw + Vortex Channeling |

At level **50**, the two final branches (Moon Essence Cross / Evolution Stone) unlock automatically.

## Costs & Limits

* Actives depend on moisture; long time out of water causes dryness weakness.
* Axolotl-based body: no helmet, amplified land moisture drain (1.5× before "Become Water").
