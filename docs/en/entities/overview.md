# Creatures

## Witch Familiar

Witch Familiars can appear with raid witches or spawn in suitable dark areas of the Overworld. An ownerless familiar may find and follow a nearby visible witch. It belongs to the raider faction and uses a blue fire ring against nearby enemies. [MCMod entry](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/969699.html)

## Wild Axolotl Shifter

A neutral amphibious creature found in lush cave waters. It can also spawn near vanilla axolotls. Moisture affects its maximum health, and it seeks water when dry. It can use Water Burst in combat. [MCMod entry](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/987007.html)

## Lunar Spirit

[Summon Lunar Spirit](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/999903.html) calls companions that follow you and shoot homing light bolts. By default, spell tiers 1–2 summon one, tiers 3–4 summon two, and tier 5 summons three. At most six loaded spirits per caster are kept in the current dimension. New spirits replace the oldest when necessary. Spirits can disappear early if the owner dies, disconnects, or changes dimension. [MCMod entry](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/999889.html)

## Orcafis (8.0.1 development build)

The `8.0.1-dev.15` code registers Orcafis and its spawn egg. It has 300 base health and attacks on land and in water. Use the spawn egg in the development build's Creative inventory to see it.
